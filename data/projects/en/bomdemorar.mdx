## What it does

BomDeMorar lets real residents review neighborhoods on safety, cost-benefit, mobility, and infrastructure, helping people find the best place to live.

## How to use

Visit [bomdemorar.com](https://www.bomdemorar.com), search for a neighborhood, read resident reviews, and compare scores across categories. If you live there, leave a review to help others.

Useful filters:

- Compare safety scores between two districts before moving.
- Read mobility comments to understand commute reality.
- Filter by family-friendly infrastructure.
